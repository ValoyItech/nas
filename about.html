Who we are

Specialist help for everyday key and lock problems.

National Auto Service Key provides automotive key cutting, key and remote programming, ECU programming, key reader replacement and locksmith services. We serve customers through our listed locations in Malamulele and Pretoria.

We understand that a missing key, faulty remote or lock problem can interrupt your day. Our aim is to make it easier to explain the issue, share the right details and get in touch with us.
