Common questions

Good to know

Can you help if I have lost all my car keys?

Send your vehicle make, model, year and location. We can discuss the available options and confirm whether your vehicle can be supported.

Can you program a replacement remote?

Remote programming is one of our listed services. Vehicle compatibility and the type of remote will need to be checked.

Do you work on house keys as well?

Yes. Household locksmith services are also offered. Contact us and describe the lock or key issue.

Do you cover both Malamulele and Pretoria?

Both locations are listed. Please contact us first to confirm availability and arrangements for your area.

Not sure which service you need?

Describe the problem and we'll help you with the next step.
