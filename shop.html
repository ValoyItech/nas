How to find your key number

Don't know your key number?

If you need a replacement key for a house, padlock, or specific lock system, we can usually cut it from the original. If you don't have the original, look for the key number stamped on the key itself or on the lock cylinder.

  • Check the head of the original key
  • Look for a small metal tag on the keyring
  • Look for a stamp on the lock face or cylinder
  • If unsure, send us a clear photo on WhatsApp

Can't find what you need?

We stock a wide range of automotive keys, batteries, and household locks. If you don't see it here, send us a message with a photo and we'll let you know if we can order it.
